Digital Inception

After the concept by Ian Jarchevski and Tyler Sauls
Draw on the room to create. Drag the screen to move it. Tap a feed post to test whether its author is human.
Shared spaceThe feed is the commons. Everyone in it looks like a user, and you only know you’re human because you’re the one typing.
Creator and creationWhat you draw or post is a creation. The layers watching it are observers, and each one is watched by the next.
Droste effectThe screen holds the room, the room holds the screen, and so on without end. Each layer is a slightly older echo of the one above.
Dead internetGoing deeper, human presence drains away. Layers blur into pixels, your drawings get remixed, and your posts come back as bot speech.
